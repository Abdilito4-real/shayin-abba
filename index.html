Our Story

Founded in 2023 in Unguwan Rimi, Kaduna, Shayin Abba was built around tea culture and community. Inspired by authentic Arabian tea ceremonies and Northern Nigerian social warmth, we created a modern sanctuary for relaxation, conversation, and nightlife.

A social lounge where every cup tells a story, and every evening glows with golden-light energy. It's not just tea — it's culture, connection, and refined taste.
